# The $V_{\mathcal B}$ Hypothesis

This repository contains the **Mathematica notebooks and supplementary code** supporting the following works:

### Published article

> **Production-Dependent Interpretation of the KLOE–MAMI (\eta \to \pi^{0}\gamma\gamma) Tension via the Nucleon-Triggered Leptophobic Vector (V_{\mathcal B})**
> Yaroslav Balytskyi
> *Journal of High Energy Physics* **2026**, 53 (2026)
> [Published article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP02%282026%29053) · [arXiv:2507.09436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09436)

### Companion preprint

> **Kinematic Fingerprints of a Nucleon-Triggered (V_{\mathcal B}) in Rare (\eta^{(\prime)}\to\pi^0(\eta)\gamma\gamma) Decays on Nucleon Targets**
> Yaroslav Balytskyi
> [arXiv:2608.13537](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13537)

The published article introduces the production-dependent (V_{\mathcal B}) mechanism as an interpretation of the KLOE–MAMI tension. The companion preprint develops its experimentally testable kinematic signatures in nucleon-produced (\eta^{(\prime)}) events.
